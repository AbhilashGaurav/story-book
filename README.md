# 🚀 Gemini Storybook 2.0 📖🎬

[![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3.11-blue)](https://www.python.org/)  
[![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-green)](LICENSE)




**Gemini Storybook 2.0** is an AI-powered storytelling pipeline that automates the creation of story videos — from text generation to audio, visuals, video assembly, and YouTube publishing.  

This is an upgraded and fully automated version of my earlier Storybook project, leveraging **LLM, TTS, AI illustrations, and video automation**.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎭 **LLM-powered story generation** – fresh, unique, and non-repetitive stories  
- 🎙️ **Voice mapping (TTS)** – character dialogues and narration brought to life  
- 🎨 **AI-generated illustrations** – visuals created from story prompts  
- 🎞️ **Video assembly** – syncs images and dialogues into smooth story clips  
- 📺 **YouTube automation** – auto-generates titles, tags, thumbnails, and uploads  

---

## 🔹 Final Output

- Fully AI-crafted storybook video (`final_story_book.mp4`)  
- Complete with its own thumbnail and metadata
